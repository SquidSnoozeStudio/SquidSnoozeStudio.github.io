Game developer, programmer, artist.

Hello! I'm SquidSnoozeStudio, an indie game developer. I love flexing my creative muscles by making games and learning more about game development. Whether I'm designing a new prototype, participating in a game jam, or chipping away on my next big project, I always try to experiment with unique ideas and maintain a professional level of quality in my work.

Background

I've been playing video games for as long as I can remember. Some of my all-time favorites include Super Mario Galaxy, Terraria, Outer Wilds, and Elden Ring. However, it wasn't until college that I really started getting into programming, which is when I discovered my love for game development! Back then, I would only ever mess around with Unity, trying to make something out of nothing with no real plan or greater ideas in mind. But eventually, I started actually designing games, planning things out, and publishing my games!

Skills

Working as an independent game developer has helped me hone many skills. I am proficient with the Unity game engine, as well as programming in C#. In addition to that, the games I've made have given me experience in level design, time management, storytelling, debugging, problem-solving, quality assurance, as well as many more skills. With every game I make, I'm not just expressing myself, I'm learning something new!

Creative Passion

Being creative is very important to me. Whether it be through building games, editing my own YouTube videos, or just making digital art for fun, I really enjoy bringing ideas to life and sharing my passions through what I make. Honestly, it feels like sometimes I've got more ideas than I know what to do with, but I believe that's what keeps me inspired.
