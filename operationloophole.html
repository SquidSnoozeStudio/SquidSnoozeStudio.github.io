Operation Loophole

My Roles: Team Lead, Game Designer, 2D Artist, Programming Assist

Dates: July 2025 - Aug 2025

“Agent… Our worst fears have been realized… We've made first contact with an alien civilization, and they do not come in peace. Thanks to the genius of Dr. Looppenheimer, however, we've equipped your car with the LOOPHOLE APPARATUS. Use it to defend the city. You're our only hope…”

This game was a group effort submission for the GMTK Game Jam 2025, finished in just 4 days. We were a team of 5, where I served as the team lead, game designer, 2D artist, and one of two programmers. In this game, you drive around in a fast car trying to close loops with your trail. Once a loop is closed, everything inside it will blow up. You can use this unique method of destruction to fend off an alien invasion (and maybe destroy some other stuff along the way).

This project was a step in a new direction for me because not only was this my first time managing a game development team, but it was also my first time shipping a complete 3D title. On top of that, I worked very closely with our other programmer. Together, we coded the game, managed our git repos, and experimented with a new scriptable-object oriented workflow in Unity.

One main takeaway I had from this was that managing a team is an endeavor all on its own. One must keep track of everyone's progress, provide meaningful feedback, and maintain high spirits to truly empower the team and create something amazing. I also learned the value of external libraries and tools when it comes to game development, especially in a time crunch like a game jam. You need to be as efficient as possible with every bit of time you have, and tools and libraries can aid greatly there.
